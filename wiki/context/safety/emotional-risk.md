# 情緒風險

## core

Emotional risk is not a chart factor; it is a safety and tone factor. If the user is anxious, self-blaming, desperate, or overwhelmed, the product must reduce certainty, avoid blame, avoid behavior prediction, and keep action advice low-pressure. (claims: context-emotional-risk-001, context-emotional-risk-002)

For self-blaming users, the reading should translate patterns without assigning all responsibility to the user. For desperate or overwhelmed users, it should prioritize stabilization and boundaries before relationship action. (claims: context-emotional-risk-001, context-emotional-risk-003)

## in_relationship

When the relationship is still active, emotional risk should prevent the product from turning pressure into accusation. It may name a difficult interaction cycle, but it should not tell the user that they alone caused the crisis. (claims: context-emotional-risk-001)

## in_breakup

After breakup or no-contact, emotional risk should lower the confidence of "contact now" advice. The product can explain chart climate, but it should not encourage repeated checking, panic messaging, or indefinite waiting. (claims: context-emotional-risk-002, context-emotional-risk-003)
