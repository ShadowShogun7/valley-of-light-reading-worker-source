# 聯絡狀態

## core

Contact status is a real-world gate. The same chart evidence should produce different advice if the two people are still chatting, occasionally replying, fully no-contact, living/working together, or blocked. Astrology may describe the climate around communication, but it cannot guarantee a response. (claims: context-contact-status-001, context-contact-status-002, context-contact-status-004)

When contact is blocked or fully absent, the reading should lower action certainty and avoid telling the user to push for an answer. When contact still exists, the reading can discuss low-pressure communication conditions, while still avoiding guaranteed outcome language. (claims: context-contact-status-001, context-contact-status-003)

The action scale should be state-specific: blocked means no outreach, no-contact means observation or one low-stimulation test only when calculation and timing support it, occasional replies mean response-led pacing, cold live contact means repair inside the existing channel, and shared-space contact means protecting the common environment before relationship discussion. (claims: context-contact-status-004, context-contact-status-005, context-contact-status-006, context-contact-status-007, context-contact-status-008)

## in_relationship

If there is still daily-life contact, contact status should shift the answer toward tone, timing, and repair conditions rather than "will they come back." If contact is cold but present, the product can suggest lowering pressure without giving a full message script on the free page. (claims: context-contact-status-003)

## in_breakup

If contact is absent or blocked, contact status should act as a hard reducer modifier: do not convert attraction into immediate action advice, and do not imply that a timing window overrides the person's boundary. If the user is blocked, the reading must not suggest bypass routes such as alternate accounts, friends, or engineered shared-space pressure. (claims: context-contact-status-001, context-contact-status-002, context-contact-status-004)

**Claim:** No-contact status lowers action speed; timing support can only become observation or one short, low-stimulation, easy-exit test when the chart evidence also supports repair.
