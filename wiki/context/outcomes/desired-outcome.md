# 用戶想要的結果

## core

Desired outcome tells the reducer what the user is trying to get from the reading: reconnect, decide, understand, release, or stabilize. The product should not treat all outcomes as the same. Someone who wants to reconnect needs conditional repair and timing boundaries; someone who wants to decide needs a healthier wait/release frame; someone who wants to understand needs pattern explanation without blame. (claims: context-desired-outcome-001, context-desired-outcome-002)

Desired outcome should influence wording and paid unlock direction, but it must not override chart evidence, contact status, or safety boundaries. Wanting reunion does not make reunion likely; wanting release should not erase real grief or attachment. (claims: context-desired-outcome-001, context-desired-outcome-003)

## in_relationship

In an active relationship, desired outcome separates repair from decision. The same pressure evidence can mean "slow down and talk honestly" for a repair user, but "check whether this is still healthy" for a decide user. (claims: context-desired-outcome-002)

## in_breakup

After breakup, desired outcome should prevent the answer from defaulting to reunion. The reading can support reconnect, understand, decide, or release, but each path needs its own boundaries. (claims: context-desired-outcome-001, context-desired-outcome-003)
