# 曖昧未定期

## core

Ambiguous stage means the relationship has not been clearly defined. The reading should avoid breakup language unless the user has explicitly described a breakup, and it should avoid commitment language unless real-world behavior supports it. Astrology can describe attraction, curiosity, communication, and pressure, but it cannot decide the relationship label for the two people. (claims: context-stage-ambiguous-001, context-stage-ambiguous-002)

In this stage, "do they love me" and "is there a chance" should be translated into safer questions: is there mutual responsiveness, is the communication consistent, does pressure rise when clarity is requested, and can the connection move from chemistry into defined behavior. (claims: context-stage-ambiguous-001)

## in_relationship

For an undefined bond, the product should describe signals as potential and conditions. It may say there is attraction or a communication opening, but it should not call the person an ex, promise commitment, or imply that waiting silently will define the relationship. (claims: context-stage-ambiguous-001, context-stage-ambiguous-002)
