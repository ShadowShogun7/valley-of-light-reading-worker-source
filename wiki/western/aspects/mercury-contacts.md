# 水星溝通相位

## core

Mercury contacts are the relationship layer for words, interpretation, curiosity, argument style, and whether two people can make their inner experience understandable to each other. They should not be treated as proof of love or proof of breakup, but they are essential when the user asks why messages became cold, why the other person misunderstood, or whether contact could be reopened. (claims: western-aspects-mercury-contacts-001)

Mercury-Mercury contacts are the direct mental-compatibility layer: soft or conjunction contacts can support shared interests and easier conversation, while hard contacts can describe different approaches to the same problem. This should change the communication reading, not become a verdict about the whole relationship. (claims: western-aspects-mercury-contacts-006)

Moon-Mercury contacts are especially useful when the emotional layer and verbal layer do not move at the same speed. A difficult Moon-Mercury contact can describe a pattern where one person is speaking from feeling while the other is answering from logic, so the product should translate the mismatch rather than blame either person. (claims: western-aspects-mercury-contacts-002)

Mercury-Mars contacts can sharpen conversation. In easy forms this can create fast exchange and useful challenge; in hard forms, the same speed can become defensiveness, irritation, or the feeling that every message is a fight. (claims: western-aspects-mercury-contacts-003)

Mercury-Saturn contacts can stabilize thinking and bring realism, but they can also make communication slow, judged, or over-edited. In breakup and cold-war readings, this is a strong candidate for "why they do not answer directly" without claiming that silence proves love. (claims: western-aspects-mercury-contacts-004)

Mercury-Venus contacts support pleasant or affectionate conversation, but they still need pressure and emotional safety context. A sweet way of speaking can lower tension; it does not guarantee commitment or repair. (claims: western-aspects-mercury-contacts-005)

## in_relationship

In an active relationship, Mercury contacts explain how two people negotiate repair after a trigger: whether they can name the issue, whether criticism lands as attack, whether affection can be verbalized, and whether a practical conversation helps or shuts one person down. Use this layer for "what happened between us" and "how should we talk" questions, while keeping message templates for the paid report. (claims: western-aspects-mercury-contacts-001, western-aspects-mercury-contacts-003, western-aspects-mercury-contacts-004)

### 優勢

- Can explain why a short message changes the emotional climate quickly. (claims: western-aspects-mercury-contacts-001)
- Can separate communication mismatch from lack of feeling. (claims: western-aspects-mercury-contacts-002)
- Can show whether a low-pressure conversation has a realistic opening. (claims: western-aspects-mercury-contacts-005)

### 弱點

- Hard Mercury contacts can make both people feel they are defending themselves instead of being heard. (claims: western-aspects-mercury-contacts-003, western-aspects-mercury-contacts-004)
- Mercury evidence alone cannot decide whether the relationship should continue. (claims: western-aspects-mercury-contacts-001)

## in_breakup

In breakup, cold-war, or no-contact scenarios, Mercury contacts should be used to identify the communication gate: whether silence is more likely pressure, misunderstanding, mental overload, or fear of saying the wrong thing. The free page may describe the gate and the risk of pushing too hard; it should not give a full message script or a guaranteed response date. (claims: western-aspects-mercury-contacts-001, western-aspects-mercury-contacts-003, western-aspects-mercury-contacts-004)
